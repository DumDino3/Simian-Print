---- INTRODUCTION ----
This is a prototype of the game Simian Print, it's purpose is to test the movement mechanics.

------ CONTROLS ------
WHILE NOT GRABBED ON PIVOT:
[A and D (Left and Right arrow)] move left and right respectively
[Space] jump
[Hold Space near a Purple Pivot] Grab on Pivot

WHILE GRABBED ON PIVOT:
[Release SPACE] drop down
[Hold A or D (Left or Right arrow)] swing left and right
[Hold W or Up arrow (can be combined with holding A or D, similarly Left and Right arrow)] Swing upwards

IMPORTANT:
At any point in the game, you can press R to restart 

-------- TIP ---------
If you feel like the movement is too hard, take it slow, hold Space without any movement input to stay still, collect yourself, then swing!

---- KNOWN ISSUES ----
BUGS:
- Player sometimes clip through the platforms if they fall into them at a certain angle.
- Spamming space at a pivot sometimes causes the game to crash.
